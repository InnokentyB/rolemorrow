Start with one role worth exploring.

You can save vacancy links and prepare a packet without running a live AI search. A fictional example lets you explore the workflow without sending your profile to a model provider.

  1. Save your profile and goal

    Use confirmed facts from your experience. Keep your target role and work preferences separate from what you have already done.

  2. Choose employer sources

    Add official careers pages or save a vacancy link. Live search shows source coverage, including what could not be checked.

  3. Review before you act

    Compare job requirements with cited profile evidence. Save a role and prepare a versioned application packet for your own review.

Know what leaves your workspace.

A live check asks for approval to send your saved profile and goal to Nebius, and search queries to Tavily. Saving a packet does not send it.

Email sending requires a reviewed recipient, saved content and your explicit confirmation. Rolemorrow does not complete employer application forms for you.

Public beta

Sending is currently limited to the owner’s test address.

New accounts can explore search, save roles and prepare packets. General employer email sending is not available yet.
